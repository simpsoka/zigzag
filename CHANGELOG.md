# Changelog

## 0.1.3 — 2026-11-13

- Choose your machine: nine built in, and your own in the new Settings
  window, each with its hoops, file format, shortest stitch and lock stitches.
- Update from inside the app: ZigZag finds new releases on GitHub and, on a
  Mac, installs them and reopens. Also installable with Homebrew.
- Satin lettering from filled letters: shapes are cut into columns where they
  branch, with an adjustable lean.
- Keep travel out of sight: fills sew their sections in the order that hides
  it, and the thread is cut (or, by a setting, sewn over the fill) otherwise.
- Lock and hide objects, and drag a box to select; trackpad scrolling pans.
- More hoop sizes and a custom hoop; a shortest stitch and lock styles for
  machines that need them.
- For agents: a log of every tool call, terse answers, objects named by the
  ids they were given, and fixes across the tools.
