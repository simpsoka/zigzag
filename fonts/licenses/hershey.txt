Hershey Sans and Hershey Script: the Hershey Fonts, from the SVG fonts
prepared by Windell H. Oskay (www.evilmadscientist.com) as shipped with
Inkscape (https://gitlab.com/inkscape/extensions, svg_fonts/), adapted
from emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey by way of "Hershey Fonts in
SVG" by Marty McGuire. Converted to TrueType for ZigZag (each stroke
written out and back along itself) by tools/fonts/single_line.py.

The Hershey Fonts are a set of vector fonts with a liberal license.
