# ZigZag 0.1.11

Layers shows what each layer is: a small picture of it beside its name,
in rows laid out as Illustrator's. Groups can be changed (layers dragged
into one, out of one, two merged; Group and Ungroup), docked panels are
as tall as you make them, and VP3 files open.

## Layers

- Each row has a **picture of its object**, in its thread color on the
  color of the cloth, beside its stitch type; a group's row shows its
  layers together. The pictures are drawn once and kept, so a long list
  scrolls as it did.
- Rows are laid out as in Illustrator: a group's arrow in a column at the
  left, the pictures in a column for each level, a group's layers set in
  past its own picture. A group nobody named reads **Group**.
- A group whose layers have others sewn between them is listed as a row
  for each run of its own, as before; now each row is of the layers
  under it. Its picture and stitch count are theirs, a click on it (or on
  one of them on the canvas) selects them and not the rest of the group,
  and it opens, hides, locks and drags on its own.

## Groups

- **Drag a layer into a group**: onto the group's row, which lights up,
  or among its layers. Drag it beside a layer that's in no group to take
  it out. Drag one group's row onto another to make one group of the two.
- **Object › Group** (⌘G) makes a group of the selection, sewn together
  where the last of them was; **Ungroup** (⇧⌘G) takes the selection out
  of its group. Both are in the layer menu too.
- A layer moved between groups stays where it is on the cloth, keeps its
  color and outline, and keeps its place in the sewing order unless it
  was dropped somewhere else in the list.
- Artwork that's added (placed, traced, or drawn by an agent) no longer
  arrives as one group of everything in it. The groups the artwork has
  of its own are kept.

## Panels

- **A docked panel's bottom edge sets its height**: drag it, and
  double-click it to have the panel fit what's in it again. The heights
  are remembered. The Layers list is as tall as it's made; a panel of
  settings made shorter scrolls, and dragged near its full height takes
  all of it.
- The mouse wheel over a panel made short scrolls the dock, so a short
  panel doesn't catch the wheel on the way past.
- **The Stitch panel keeps one height** whichever layer is selected, so
  the panels under it stay where they are as you click from a fill to a
  satin and back. It scrolls for the settings that don't fit, and its
  edge drags to the height you'd rather it had.

## Machine files

- **VP3** files (Husqvarna Viking, Pfaff) open, as PES, PEC, DST, EXP and
  JEF do: a block for each thread, in its own color. One that was cut
  short opens with what's there.
- **File › Place takes a machine file**: its stitches come into the
  design that's open as manual stitches, at the size they were digitized,
  a group for each color.

## Working with an agent

- An agent is told how to group what it draws (the parts of one thing
  together, named for it, and nothing else), sees which group each object
  is in, and can group and ungroup objects (`group_objects`,
  `ungroup_objects`).

## Windows

- ZigZag opens without a console window behind it. Started from a
  terminal, it still prints `--help` and its logs there.
