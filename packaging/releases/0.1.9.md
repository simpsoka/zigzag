# ZigZag 0.1.9

The agent looks a design over as an embroiderer would before it calls it
done; trim settings you choose stay as you chose them; fills on standard
fabric are pull-compensated; the Agent panel steps aside while you work;
and a recently opened tab shows its stitches at once.

## Working with an agent

- Before it finishes, the agent reviews the design for what an
  embroiderer checks first: underlay under what needs it, rows close
  enough to cover the fabric (a lighter background only where the fabric
  is close to its thread), fills that don't run the same way as what they
  sit on, satin underlay that suits the column, shapes sewn only as an
  outline, a patch border sewn last and wide enough, lettering at least
  5 mm tall (6.5 mm on a cap), lines sewn across a plain background, and
  specks a trace left behind. It suggests satin only for real bands, not
  for lobed flowers and leaves.
- Sew over fill, Connect and Blend that you set are your trade-off for
  fewer trims: the agent leaves them alone and says what they cost, and
  only asks to undo one it turned on itself. A gap sewn across counts as
  showing only where its thread stands out.
- The floating Agent panel's ≡ menu has **Auto-Hide**, on unless you turn
  it off: clicking the canvas or picking a tool puts the panel away, and
  the Agent button brings it back.
- Hover a queued message for **Send Now**: the turn running stops and
  that message goes at once; the rest still wait.
- The prompt keeps one placeholder and its height while the agent works,
  so it no longer jumps as you type.
- On/off items in panel menus are checkboxes at the right of their row,
  and the whole row toggles them.

## Stitching

- Fills on standard fabric are pull-compensated by 0.2 mm, so they meet
  the satin or fill beside them without a gap. Rows widened for it stay
  out of narrow cuts and necks in the shape.

## Issues

- The thread that Sew over fill or Connect sews in sight is no longer
  flagged: it's the trade you chose. Travel the planner couldn't hide on
  its own still is.

## Fixes

- A recently opened tab shows its stitches as soon as it comes forward
  (its last plan is kept beside its recovery file), instead of after a
  wait of up to a second or more.
